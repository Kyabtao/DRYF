Open-Your-Store Checklist ✅

Use 🧺 Store List on any product (in the cart drawer) to build your opening stock list, then download it as CSV.

1

📍 Location & Licences

High-street / market shop, FSSAI licence, GST registration, trade licence & current account.

2

🤝 Suppliers

Wholesale mandis (e.g. Khari Baoli, Delhi) & 2–3 importers. Compare rates weekly.

3

🫙 Storage & Display

Airtight glass jars, cool dry shelves, FIFO rotation, weighing & sealing machines.

4

🏷️ Pack Sizes

100g / 250g / 500g / 1kg packs — small packs bring trial buyers, family packs bring repeats.

5

🎁 Festive Gifting

Diwali, Rakhi, weddings & corporate orders = biggest profit season.

6

📱 Sell Online

This same demo design can become your real store on WhatsApp, Google & marketplaces.
