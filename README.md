# DRYF — Dry Fruits, Seeds & Chocolates Store Catalogue

An animated, interactive website listing **98 items** you can sell in a dry fruits store.

## What's inside
- **6 categories:** Dry Fruits & Nuts (22) · Berries & Dried Fruits (16) · Seeds & Makhana (16) · Chocolates (20) · Roasted & Masala Snacks (14) · Gift Hampers & Combos (10)
- Every item has a photo, English + Hindi name, indicative price (₹/500g), shelf life, rating and a selling benefit
- **Search** (try "badam", "kaju", "chia"), category filters, importance filter (Must-Have / Popular / Premium) and sorting
- **My Store List** — tick items you want, see estimated opening-stock value, then Copy / Download CSV / Print your list (saved in your browser)
- **Starter Packs** — Essential / Complete / Premium ranges added in one click
- **Open-Your-Store checklist** and store-name ideas
- Item detail popups with storage & selling tips, scroll animations, animated counters and a scrolling item marquee

## Run it
Just open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then visit http://localhost:8000
```

> Prices are indicative retail rates for planning only — confirm with your local wholesale supplier (rates vary by city & season).
