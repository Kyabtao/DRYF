Before you launch

Open-Your-Store Checklist ✅

A quick roadmap that goes with your item list.

1

📍 Location & Licences

High-street / market shop, FSSAI food licence, GST registration, trade licence & a current account.

2

🤝 Suppliers

Source from wholesale mandis (e.g. Khari Baoli, Delhi) & 2–3 importers. Compare rates weekly — prices move with season.

3

🫙 Storage & Display

Airtight glass jars, cool dry shelves away from sunlight, FIFO rotation, weighing scale & sealing machine.

4

🏷️ Pack Sizes

Offer 100g / 250g / 500g / 1kg packs. Small packs bring trial customers; family packs bring repeat buyers.

5

🎁 Festive & Gifting

Diwali, Rakhi, weddings & corporate orders are the biggest profit season — keep hampers ready from day one.

6

📱 Sell Online Too

List bestsellers on WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram & marketplaces for home delivery in your area.
